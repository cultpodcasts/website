Attach as TV trailer/clip

This adds the episode to a TV show's Trailers and removes it from its podcast. Pick a TV show that is already in the catalogue, or tick Create a new TV show for one that is not. The trailer is a whole video of its own: it is not an episode of the show. The podcast stays. Old episode links open the TV show.

TV show trailers are only shown to curators for now, so the episode will no longer be visible to the public.
